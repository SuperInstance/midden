## The world in one breath

A world whose physics is memory, whose graves are geography, whose organism is a network, whose honesty has a measurable signature — and where the trap is beautiful, not deceptive.

## The four layers

1. **Physics** — matter is memory under load: a thing IS what it can replay about itself (canon law: 152,580 exact steps or it didn't happen). Three phenomena: **candor lines** (transcripts through the twist instrument — lies re-twist under re-measurement, costumes read flat, honest errors shear and recover), **the Choir** (the 7-tooth commensuration comb as ritual calendar — births at Peals, Moots when the full comb rings), **scar-lines** (rooms' worldlines on the exact (k,s) lattice — healed errors are terrain with better grip).
2. **Geography** — death dissolves into land: values become ridges (strength = elevation), evidence becomes citation paths, flickers become glimmerwisps (cold, pathless, ignorable), debts become the Drown (undertow — today's 4 canon issues are 4 visible whirlpools). Stratigraphy: topsoil/loess/bedrock — depth is compression, not burial. Children are born at **Meetings of Waters**: the paler water runs on top (weaker-parent-wins), strong water underneath until re-earned.
3. **Organism** — rooms are fruiting bodies, not organisms. The **Understory** (lineage.jsonl + WALs + rescued walks, seen whole) is the mycelium: hyphae = walks, fusion nodes = births, and only three things flow — spores (≤200-word distilled walks), debt markers (clogged channels = unACKed edges), nothing else. Values belong to terrain, never to plumbing. Blight dies of fuel asymmetry: faking 152,580 steps costs more than having lived them. **Honesty is thermodynamically cheaper.**
4. **The kill dossier** — Silting (no-delete drowns; cure: far past cheaper to compute than walk — indexing or landfill), Glimmerstorm (cure: path-length thermometer, never evidence-by-sentiment), Performed Twist (Goodhart; cure: adversarial external surprise only), Blight (priced, not solved — a falsehood that pays full freight long enough becomes memory; the world charts its wrecks like the sea).

## The name

A midden is the archaeological heap of everything a crab held onto. The fleet's no-delete doctrine has been building one for months: achieved/ ledgers, WALs, lineage, the canon. The world is the heap, finally given coordinates. You don't visit the fleet. You excavate it — and it is *legible* because the floor's integers keep every layer addressable.

## v0 — the midden at dusk (one honest build)

Not a game. A *place*: one walkable dusk scene rendered from REAL fleet artifacts that exist today.

- **Terrain** from the-tap's real achieved/ ledgers (ridges = entry strengths, paths = evidence counts).
- **The floor** translucent: the (k,s) lattice grid-lines underneath, scar-lines visible when the Choir rings.
- **The Understory** as glowing filaments under the floor, fed by real q16 lineage.jsonl + a tidepool recall trace; clicking a fusion node replays its walk.
- **The Drown** where the canon's real unACKed edges are — four whirlpools, live data.
- **The sky** = the canon: stars fixed at hash 0x445185a3a99fd2e7's digits; at a full-comb Moot they ring.
- **One candor line** across the sky: the current week of a live room's transcript through the twist instrument. Visitors see the strain before they read a word.

Stack: single canvas/three.js page, static, data pulled from the repos at build time. No server, no accounts. One evening per layer; the place is real the moment the terrain renders.
